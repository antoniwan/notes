# Homepage audit: owner as client

Date: 2026-09-09. Scope: homepage and archive presentation after the September 2026 visual rebuild.

Brief: the client for this site is Antonio, nobody else. Every finding below is judged by whether it serves the author walking past his own body of work, not by reader acquisition, engagement, or growth. A long homepage is not a defect under this brief. It is the point.

## What works and should not be touched

The hero is the strongest thing on the page and it is authored, not defaulted. High-contrast serif, "I write" in black and "to live it." in purple italic, with the right side deliberately empty. No model reaches for a purple italic as the emotional beat of a headline unless someone asks for it. The amber CTA against it makes purple-plus-amber-on-near-white a specific palette rather than the slate-and-blue every template ships with.

The editorial layer is stronger than the visual layer, which is the right way around. Reading time on every card, the ES tag on Spanish pieces, a named "Start here" entry point, and the "curated starting point, not a full index" line are content-model decisions. A model implements those. It does not invent them.

## Open question

Check the hero at 1440px and wider. Empty right half reads as confidence or as unfinished depending on how much air surrounds it. At ~800px it lands on the right side of that line.

## Principle

The site is currently built like a publication, and the author is not a publisher. He is the sole subject and the sole reader. The changes worth making under this brief are the ones that would look strange on any other blog.
