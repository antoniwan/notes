## The belief

Feedback is king for everything I do. Bringing in people who know a topic better than I do, and hearing what they think, flattering or not, positive or negative, always levels up whatever they touch.

## Where it comes from

Work taught it to me, over and over. Some of the best leaps I've made came from a single comment by a colleague who saw something I couldn't. Lately I've been learning from both younger and older peers at the same job, and I keep asking what changed: am I wiser, or just listening better?

## How it shows up for me

I ask for reviews on almost everything, including the writing on this site. I try to treat a hard note as a gift, because somebody spent their attention on my work.

I always listen, yes. But I can listen better, and listen more. I still get defensive in the first minute, and I'm still working on letting that minute pass before I answer.
