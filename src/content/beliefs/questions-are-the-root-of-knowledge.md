## The belief

The root of all human knowledge is questions. Every answer we have started as somebody asking something. Without questions there's no progress, and in a way, no humanity.

## Where it comes from

I've always been the kid, and then the adult, who asks one more question. I said it out loud in a conversation in 2025 and realized it was the thing underneath my curiosity.

## How it shows up for me

I try to ask before I assume, at work, at home, and with my kids, who are much better at asking than I am.

Where I fail: once I think I already know, I stop asking. That's exactly when the question matters most.
