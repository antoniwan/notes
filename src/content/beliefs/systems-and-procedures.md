## The belief

Systems and procedures, as a law of life. If you have them, you can do whatever you want with enough willpower, consistency, time, effort and energy. Talent helps. A system keeps working on the days talent doesn't show up.

## Where it comes from

It's the one thing my dad taught me, and taught me well: _sistemas y procedimientos_. In 2016 I wrote that excelling at anything takes discipline and self-enforced systems, and that any task benefits immensely from them.

## How it shows up for me

I build a system for almost everything: cooking, money, writing, the way this site gets made. When something keeps going wrong, my first question isn't "who failed" but "what's the procedure, and where does it break."

The risk is that I can build a system for something that needed a conversation instead. Not everything in a family is a process.
