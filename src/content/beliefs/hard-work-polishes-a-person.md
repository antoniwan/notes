## The belief

Hard work polishes a person. Long, honest effort almost always turns into something good, even when you fail at the thing you were working on, because you come out of it sharper than you went in.

## Where it comes from

My parents taught it to my sister and me by doing it. I've been saying "hard work polishes a person" since at least 2014, to coworkers moving to new jobs and to younger cousins starting out.

## How it shows up for me

I like people who put in the work, and I try to be one of them. When something is hard, I treat the difficulty as part of what I'm getting, not a sign I should stop.

But I've also burned out more than once, and I've written that it felt like modern slavery. In 2020 I even wrote that success was 70% luck, 20% preparation and 10% hard work. Both things are true for me: the work polishes you, and the work can grind you down if nobody, including you, is watching the dose.
