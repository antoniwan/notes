## What it is

When something irritates you over and over, the usual answer is "be more patient." Often the real problem is the design. A two-second request can hide a fifteen-second process, and tiny taxes paid over and over compound into resentment.

You think you need more patience. Maybe you need a different system. You think your kid is being difficult. Maybe the friction is in the request, not the child.

## How it shows up for me

My daughter used to ask several times a day how much longer until I finished work. Each answer meant stopping, finding a clock, remembering my end time, subtracting, translating it for a five-year-old, and then finding my place again. The fix wasn't patience. It was a clock that counts down to the end of my workday. The irritation disappeared on its own.
