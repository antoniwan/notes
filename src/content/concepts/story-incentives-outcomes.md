## What it is

A three-part model for how powerful companies operate:

1. **Story:** what they say they are. Liberators, visionaries, fire-bringers.
2. **Incentives:** what actually pays. Ship fast, capture markets, externalize risk and privatize gains, and file the harms under "edge cases" and "user misuse."
3. **Outcomes:** what accumulates downstream, for everyone else.

When the story and the outcomes disagree, believe the incentives.

## How it shows up for me

I first wrote it about AI companies, but it works for anyone, including me. When my story about myself doesn't match what happens around me, I look at what I'm actually rewarded for.
