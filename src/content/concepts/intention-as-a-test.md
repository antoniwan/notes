## What it is

Intention, the way I use it, is a test you can run on any single step: "Is this step I'm going to take taking me closer or further from where I want to be?"

It's small on purpose. You don't have to know the whole road. You just have to check the direction of the next step.

## How it shows up for me

In 2025 I set three principles: discipline, intention, and then precision. Intention asks if I'm heading the right way. Precision asks if I'm putting the effort exactly where it belongs. I use this one most at night, when the phone is in my hand.
