## What it is

A simple test. **Efficiency** is removing steps that don't add value. **Laziness** is skipping steps that do add value, because the cost of skipping them feels abstract until it isn't.

Laziness often looks like efficiency for a while. It's just laziness that hasn't been invoiced yet.

## How it shows up for me

I learned it in code. I kept committing straight to the main branch because it felt faster. Twenty-two commits went fine. Then one broke the login, and I'd traded five seconds of making a branch for thirty minutes of cleanup.

I don't call anyone else lazy for doing the same. But I know it was laziness for me, because I knew better and skipped it anyway. In 2008 I wrote that "efficiency is intelligent laziness," and I still like that, as long as the laziness is doing the thinking.
