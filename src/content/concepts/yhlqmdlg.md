## What it is

YHLQMDLG stands for _"Yo hago lo que me da la gana,"_ Puerto Rican Spanish for "I do whatever I feel like." Bad Bunny made the acronym famous as an album title in 2020.

For me it's been a personal operating principle: radical autonomy, nobody tells me how to live. It works beautifully until you meet something that really doesn't care what you want.

## How it shows up for me

Forty years of YHLQMDLG met its first absolute limit with a newborn. A baby's needs don't negotiate. I'm still autonomous, I think, but now I choose what I give up instead of pretending I don't have to.
