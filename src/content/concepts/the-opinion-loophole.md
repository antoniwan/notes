## What it is

The opinion loophole is how a falsehood or a scam slips past criticism by filing itself under protected categories: "that's just my opinion," "it's my belief," "free speech." Once something is labeled a belief, questioning it starts to look like an attack on the person.

Opinions deserve respect. Claims about facts deserve checking, whatever they call themselves.

## How it shows up for me

I'm careful here, because I want people to keep their right to believe strange things, including me. But a fake cure sold to a sick person isn't an opinion. The test I use: does it make a factual claim someone could lose money or health over?
