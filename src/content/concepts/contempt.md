## What it is

Contempt is the judgment that something or someone is beneath you: low in worth, not worth engaging as an equal. It's cold rather than hot, more sneer than fury. It's global, aimed at a whole person or group instead of a specific act. And it closes inquiry: the verdict is in, so there's nothing left to learn.

John Gottman calls it the most corrosive of the "four horsemen" in a relationship, because it works from assumed superiority.

A simple formula: contempt is observation plus a status verdict. "This is happening" is observation. "This is happening, and they're lesser for it, and I'm above it" is contempt.

## How it shows up for me

I have more of it than I'd like, especially toward groups in the news. The test I use when I write: which clause is doing the work, the observation or the verdict? If it's the verdict, I rewrite.
