## What it is

Ben Thompson describes culture as the accumulated result of a company's successful decisions. It's what lets people act without asking permission every time, which is how a company scales. But when the world changes and those old decisions stop working, the same culture becomes a straitjacket.

What a company makes, and how it makes it, is tied up with how it's structured. You can't change one without the other.

## How it shows up for me

I've seen both sides. A team where "the way we do things" saved everyone hours, and the same team two years later defending a process nobody could explain anymore. When I hear "that's just how we do it here," I ask which decision that was, and whether it still works.
