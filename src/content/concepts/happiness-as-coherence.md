## What it is

This is my own definition, and I wrote it down in November 2025: "Happiness, universally, isn't pleasure. It is alignment." When what you believe, what you say and what you do line up, there's a kind of peace that survives even hard days.

It's why discipline, love and meaning can make people feel deeply at peace even while they're suffering. And it's why pleasure without alignment can feel so empty.

## How it shows up for me

I think every human being can live this one. It doesn't need money or luck, just honesty about what you value and the nerve to act like it. When I'm unhappy, the first thing I check now is where I'm out of line with myself.
