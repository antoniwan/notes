## What it is

The problem of evil asks whether the suffering in the world counts as an argument against God: if God is all-powerful, all-knowing and wholly good, why is there evil?

A theodicy answers by giving the reason: for each evil, it describes what would justify God in allowing it. A defense claims less. It only tries to show that God and evil aren't logically contradictory, without claiming to know God's actual reasons.

## How it shows up for me

This question is a big part of why I'm an atheist. No theodicy I read could explain a suffering child in a way I could say out loud to that child's parent. I still respect the people who keep wrestling with it.
