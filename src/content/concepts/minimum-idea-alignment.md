## What it is

An idea doesn't have to be adopted by the majority to be feasible. It just needs weak opposition. Every innovation, even low-tech ones, took real effort to spread. When there's pushback, the idea has to be stronger than the consensus against it. Minimum alignment is required; mass acceptance isn't.

## How it shows up for me

At work, I stopped trying to convince everyone before starting. I find the few people who are open, build something small that works, and let it make the argument.
