## What it is

"Signal" comes from signal processing: the useful information, compared with "noise," the irrelevant interference. Think of a radio station full of static versus one that's crystal clear.

What's high signal is personal. It depends on your level of development, on what's useful to you right now.

## How it shows up for me

A beginner's book can be high signal for me in a new field and pure noise in one I know well. I try to judge sources by what they do for me today, not by how impressive they are.
