## What it is

Accountability is keeping your values and commitments visible next to what actually happened. Practices, deadlines, public promises and other people can all give you feedback when motivation alone won't carry the work.

## How it shows up for me

I treat it as a method to test, not a personality trait. Writing in public is one of mine: once it's on this site, I can't quietly pretend I never said it.
