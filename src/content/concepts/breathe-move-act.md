## What it is

When you notice you're frozen, overthinking or overwhelmed:

1. Take slow, deep breaths until you're only inside your five senses.
2. Take a single step, in any direction. Physically move from wherever you are.

It's physical on purpose. The breath comes first. The step can be any step. The point is to act.

## How it shows up for me

I wrote it down in 2024, after one too many afternoons stuck in my own head. It still works. The step is usually to the kitchen.
