## What it is

The unwitnessed self is the part of you that only exists when no one is watching, listening or present. It isn't the ordinary need for solo time to recharge; every adult needs that. It's the parts of your identity you can't bring into a room where someone else can see them.

The trap is that those hidden parts feel like the real you. Around other people, you feel like a watered-down version of yourself, so solitude becomes the only place you feel whole. Solitude becomes your home address, and shared space becomes the visit.

## How it shows up for me

I recognize myself in this, and it's why intimacy is my hardest value. The practice is small: bringing one hidden thing into a shared room. A song I love, a drawing, a thought I'd usually only write down.
