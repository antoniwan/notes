## What it is

Say-do incoherence is a stated value that conflicts with what you actually did. It happens at every scale: a parent, a boss, a company, a government. When it breaks at the scale of institutions, trust erodes everywhere, and language turns into performance with nothing behind it.

There's a healthy loop inside it, too: preach something, fail to live it, see the gap, examine it, integrate. Most people leave the loop by lowering the standard or by no longer looking. Staying in it means continuous friction, and continuous growth.

## How it shows up for me

Dissonance doesn't make me angry so much as dizzy. That's how I know I care about this. The audit I try to run on myself: does the value I just claimed match the thing I just described doing?
