## What it is

Every hard conversation has an inherent cost. It's hard because the topic is hard. The tariff is the surcharge on top of that: the ice, the sarcasm, the silent treatment, the cold week after. It's what makes bringing things up feel expensive.

When the tariff is high, people stop importing the truth.

## How it shows up for me

I used to think I was the one paying tariffs. Then I noticed I charge them too: a tone, a sigh, a long silence. Dropping my own tariff turned out to be the fastest way to get honest conversations back.
