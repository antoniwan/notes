## What it is

Detraining is the decay of fitness when training stops or drops off. It's gentler than disuse, like bed rest or a cast, where muscle loss shows up within two or three weeks.

A few useful ideas inside it:

- **Muscle memory is physical.** Nuclei added to muscle fibers while you trained stay after you stop, so regrowth on return is faster.
- **Size goes before strength.** Early loss is mostly fluid and stored fuel, not contractile protein, so you can look smaller before you're actually weaker.
- **Relative vs. absolute strength.** Relative strength is force per unit of bodyweight; handstands test it. Absolute strength is total force; barbells test it.
- **Sarcopenia** is age-related muscle loss, roughly 0.5 to 1 percent a year after 30 or 40, faster later.

## How it shows up for me

At 40, sarcopenia stopped being a word and became a reason. I train so I can keep picking up my kids, and when life interrupts training, muscle memory is the thing that keeps me from feeling like I lost everything.
