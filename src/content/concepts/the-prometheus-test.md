## What it is

Tech companies like to sell themselves as Prometheus: liberators, visionaries, bringers of fire. The test asks three plain questions of any deployment:

- **Competence:** does it actually work, and does anyone understand how?
- **Accountability:** when it fails, who answers for it?
- **Consent:** did the people affected agree to carry the risk?

Watch for **externalized risk**, costs pushed onto users and the public, and **privatized gain**, benefits kept by the company. Watch for **opaque filters**, moderation or gating with hidden criteria, and for **policy theater**, bold proposals with no workable structure underneath.
