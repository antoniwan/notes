## What it is

Atomic task ownership is assigning responsibilities that are fully specified and can't be split further without losing meaning. "You own the dishwasher: loading, running, emptying" is atomic. "Help with the kitchen" or "anything else that comes up" is not.

A vague category leaves everyone guessing where their part starts and ends, so the work falls back on whoever notices first.

## How it shows up for me

At home with a big blended crew, "everyone helps" turned into me doing it. When each kid owned one specific thing, the arguments went down and I stopped being the default. It's the same at work: a ticket with one clear owner gets done.
