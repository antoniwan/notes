## What it is

"I deserve it" sounds like self-care. Often it's reward accounting: rest, a purchase or a game, justified by prior output. The need is real. The ledger around it is the tell. It means the scorekeeper is still running, now on your permission to rest instead of on other people's judgment.

## How it shows up for me

I say "I deserve some solo time" a lot. The need for solitude is fine; I'm an introvert and I need it. What I'm working on is taking it because I need it, not because I earned it.
