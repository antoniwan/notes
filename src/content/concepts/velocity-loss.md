## What it is

Velocity loss is the distress that comes from a sudden drop in speed, agency and self-directed action. One week you're moving fast and deciding everything. The next, a newborn, an injury, a layoff or a court date has you waiting on other people's timelines.

It can look like irritability or depression. Underneath, it's grief for your own momentum.

## How it shows up for me

Paternity leave hit me this way. I love my kids, and I still felt caged by how slow everything became. Naming it as velocity loss helped me stop blaming the baby, or myself, and find small places where I could still move fast.
