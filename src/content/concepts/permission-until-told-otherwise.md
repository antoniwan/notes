## What it is

Many people wait for a spec, a sign-off, or an explicit go-ahead before starting. Others operate from the opposite default: I have permission until someone tells me I don't. They start, do good work up front, and let the quality force the conversation.

The difference in speed can be huge, because often the permission already arrived and nobody noticed.

## How it shows up for me

This is how I work, and it has served me well. It also has edges I'm careful with: it fits reversible work, and it's wrong for anything that can't be undone or that belongs to someone else. I've never tested whether it can be taught.
