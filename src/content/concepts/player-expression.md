## What it is

Player expression is the depth of a game's systems that lets each player choose how to play: how to fight, how to move across the map, how to look, how to make money. A game with high player expression feels like yours, because two people can play it very differently and both be right.

## How it shows up for me

It's the recurring theme in the games I love, and the thing I'm chasing in the game I'm building. My test of depth: I'd used maybe sixty percent of one game's combat system and was still having a great time. If a system is that fun at sixty percent, it's probably very well designed at one hundred.
