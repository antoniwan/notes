## What it is

Fundamentals are the basic techniques or core principles everything else builds on: the building blocks of competence. You wouldn't build a house on a fragile foundation, but we do it with skills all the time, skipping ahead to the advanced stuff because the basics feel boring.

## How it shows up for me

At some point I started going back to basics for everything: cleaning, hygiene, mental health, cooking, weightlifting, work, relationships, learning. It's changed more than any advanced technique ever did.

In cooking it looks like this: choosing the best ingredient for each part of the recipe, with care and attention. That's cooking with love.
