## What it is

A lot of miscommunication is a mode mismatch, not a content problem. Three ideas help:

- **Throughput:** how fast you express things.
- **Synchronization:** shared pacing, both people at the same place in the conversation.
- **Latency:** an intentional pause before responding, so what you say actually lands.

Optimizing for throughput, fast and correct and complete, can push alignment down. The intent is precision. The impact is that people feel excluded and unheard.

## How it shows up for me

This one is me. My old pattern was to think and answer in one burst. The new one is sequential: listen, understand, process, then respond. It's slower, it lowers friction, and it reads as care, which is what I meant all along.

Part of it is calibration: learning which forms of communication actually land with a particular person, versus what feels natural for me to say.
