## What it is

Happiness isn't a baseline you reach and keep. It's moments. You're a happy person because you have moments of happiness, and as you mature, you catch more of them.

There's a gentler version too: every moment offers a small glimmer, and the glimmer is given, not produced. Your effort goes into noticing it, not earning it.

## How it shows up for me

I wrote a whole essay about this. The trap for me is approving the glimmer later, at my nightly gratitude practice, instead of receiving it in the moment. I'm trying to catch it while it's happening.
