## What it is

Parentification is a collapse of roles in a family: a child takes on responsibilities that don't fit their age and starts doing a parent's job. Ivan Boszormenyi-Nagy and Geraldine Spark first defined it in 1973, from family systems theory.

It can be practical (raising younger siblings, running the house) or emotional (being a parent's confidant, keeping the peace). Some researchers count it as a form of neglect. It can also leave real strengths behind, which is part of why it's hard to see.

## How it shows up for me

The person who finally told me to get help was a teenager, a kid who shouldn't have had to carry that. It still humbles me. I try hard now to keep the adult jobs in my house with the adults.
