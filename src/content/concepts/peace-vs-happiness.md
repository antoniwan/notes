## What it is

Peace and happiness get used as synonyms, and I think they're different. Peace is a ground state: steady, quiet, not needing anything to change. Happiness comes in spikes, and when you chase it, it can turn into a performance.

The two fit together: focus on peace, and happiness tends to show up on its own, without the grasping.

## How it shows up for me

I worked this out on a January 1st, which feels right. I'd spent years chasing happy moments and feeling empty between them. Aiming for peace made the space between the spikes livable.
