## What it is

Dissociation is a spectrum, and most of it is everyday:

- **Everyday.** Highway hypnosis, losing an hour to a screen, autopilot through something boring, getting absorbed in a book. Nearly everyone does it daily.
- **Stress-driven.** Depersonalization, feeling unreal or watching yourself from outside, and derealization, the world going flat or dreamlike. Common under exhaustion or conflict, and usually temporary.
- **Clinical.** Dissociative disorders involve real memory gaps, identity disruption and trouble functioning, and are usually linked to trauma. That's a different thing, and it shouldn't be self-diagnosed from a journal entry.

## How it shows up for me

When I use the word, I mean the first kind: the hour that disappears into a phone, the drive I don't remember. Naming it helps me notice when I'm checking out instead of resting.

If the stress-driven or clinical kinds sound familiar, that's worth talking about with a professional.
