## What it is

What we do, not what we lecture, is what echoes back from our children. Habits and inclinations get installed by example. If I want my kids to be excellent and happy, I have to practice excellence and work on being a balanced, fulfilled person myself.

Discipline belongs here too, and it isn't the same as punishment. Discipline is not nasty or disrespectful. It's teaching.

## How it shows up for me

It's the most humbling idea I know as a parent, because my kids copy my phone habits and my tone of voice far more faithfully than my speeches. When I want them to change something, I check whether I'm doing it first.
