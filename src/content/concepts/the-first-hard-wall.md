## What it is

For people who've solved most things with effort, the first hard wall is a shock: a limit that work can't move. Some limits aren't puzzles. They're just facts.

## How it shows up for me

Mine came when my son was four days old. I couldn't breastfeed him, however hard I worked at being useful. I thought autonomy and capability were the same thing. _La primera pared dura aparece exactamente donde vive el nuevo amor._ The first hard wall appears exactly where the new love lives.

So I give what I can: I hold him after every feeding. It's not nothing. It just isn't everything.
