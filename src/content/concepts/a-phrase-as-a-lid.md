## What it is

A phrase is what you reach for when you don't want to do the whole thing, really. Families pass down little sentences, used for everything, that end a moment instead of meeting it. The same words can be a gift or a lid depending on why you say them.

## How it shows up for me

Mine is _"está bueno, está bueno."_ It can mean: you are enough, right now, at rest, nothing owed. It can also mean: okay, that's enough, please bring whatever you're feeling into a range I can be around. That second one is a lid.

No better phrase fixes it. A phrase is never going to replace sitting on the floor at 8 p.m. on a Wednesday with a kid who is coming apart. My kids also need to hear me say _"esto no está bueno,"_ out loud and with care.

## Try this

Notice which phrase you reach for. Then notice what you were skipping when you reached for it.
