## What it is

Discernment is precision of perception. It's the trained ability to notice fine distinctions: truth and falsehood, signal and noise, gold and fool's gold.

It's not the same as judgment. Judgment is reaching a conclusion. Discernment is the noticing that comes before it. It needs honesty with yourself, slow seeing before reacting, and lots of feedback over time.

## How it shows up for me

When I'm tired or rushed, my discernment is the first thing to go, and I start reacting to the loudest thing instead of the truest thing. Slowing down is how I get it back.
