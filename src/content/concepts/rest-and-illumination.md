## What it is

These two are easy to confuse.

**Rest** is the body settling because it feels safe.

**Illumination without integration** is the body settling because you temporarily clocked out of being you: a beautiful high, a flash of insight, a perfect escape. It's more elegant than denial, and it's still an escape. You come back to the same life unchanged.

## How it shows up for me

I can chase illumination: a long run, a great idea at midnight, a spiritual moment. They feel like rest and leave me just as tired. Real rest is boring by comparison, and it's the one that actually refills me.
