## What it is

Reality testing is the ability to tell inner experience from outer events, and to check your experience against a reality other people share. In clinical work it's a core capacity; when it slips, a person can lose track of what's real.

In everyday life it's simpler: "Is this happening, or am I telling myself it's happening?" And then, ideally, asking someone you trust.

## How it shows up for me

With AI tools in my day, I've started doing small reality checks on purpose: I run a big conclusion past a person who knows me before I act on it. Machines are agreeable. Friends are better at saying "wait."
