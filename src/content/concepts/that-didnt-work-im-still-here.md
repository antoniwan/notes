## What it is

With kids, the message underneath every correction can be the same five words: _that didn't work, I'm still here._ The behavior gets addressed. The relationship doesn't go anywhere. Mistakes don't cost connection.

It works inward too. When the inner voice starts drafting a sentence for a failure, ask one question: what did this reveal? Not "what do I owe?"

## How it shows up for me

I aim for "ever," and it's harder than it sounds. I'm not perfect at it. But my kids know the five words, and sometimes they say them back to me.
