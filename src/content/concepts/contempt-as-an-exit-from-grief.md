## What it is

"People suck" can feel like a judgment, but often it's grief looking for an exit. You take in harm you can't do anything about, the moral energy has nowhere to go, and a verdict on a whole category ends both the inquiry and the obligation.

The targeting isn't about guilt. It's about proximity. The charge lands on whatever group of people is nearest and easiest to abstract, even if they did nothing.

## How it shows up for me

I noticed it in myself. Asked what had happened in the ten minutes before the reflex rose, the answer was always intake: the news, a crime, a war I can't stop. Then some random group, parents, drivers, men, took the hit.

Now, when I feel it coming, I try to ask what I'm grieving instead. It's usually sadder, and more honest.
