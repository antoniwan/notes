## What it is

**Empathy** is understanding what someone else feels. **Compassion** is acting to ease their suffering. **Self-compassion** is turning that same care toward yourself.

How can I offer compassion to others if I won't use it to dignify myself as a human being?

## How it shows up for me

In moments of conflict or agony, I ask myself the questions I'd ask a friend:

- How would I treat a friend in this situation?
- What am I feeling right now, and is it okay to feel this way?
- Am I being too harsh on myself?
- What do I need right now?
- Is it possible that I made a mistake?
- What can I learn from this?

It helps me understand what I need, and why I'm frustrated.
