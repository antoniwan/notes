## What it is

In video games, an NPC, a non-player character, runs on a script. It says the same lines and walks the same path no matter what you do. As a metaphor, it's a person living on inertia, reacting instead of deciding.

It's harsh when you aim it at other people. It's useful when you aim it at yourself.

## How it shows up for me

I wrote in 2024: "I had stood by like a lifeless NPC, fumbling through a river of bland decisions and reactions, floating in what I felt was inertia and complacency." And: "NPCs go around scripted and being inconsequential. I was done with that and got the help I needed."
