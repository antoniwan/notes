## What it is

A lot of modern life feeds the animal that consumes, scrolls and buys, and starves the animal that creates, attunes and rests. The old split between "intelligence" and "animality" doesn't match how bodies actually work. The move isn't transcending the animal. It's curating it: choosing which mammal patterns to feed and which to put boundaries around.

## How it shows up for me

One night I wheeled my baby's crib next to me, read his fussiness, and watched him settle. That wasn't instinct as opposed to intelligence. It was mammalian intelligence working at full strength, and it felt more human than anything I'd done all day.
