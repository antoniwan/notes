## What it is

A set of principles for leading with force and love together:

- **Mercy through the blade:** exposing illusions and weakness through firm action, without hatred or cruelty.
- **Loyalty to truth, not comfort:** speaking and living the truth even when it causes temporary discomfort.
- **Offer power once:** sharing your strength openly, without attachment to whether others accept it.
- **Strike without hatred:** acting decisively when needed, without letting anger corrupt the act.
- **Cut without regret:** trusting that hard boundaries, even painful ones, can be an act of love.
- **Leave the door open, keep walking:** compassion means letting others find you when they're ready; discipline means you don't wait for them.

## How it shows up for me

I wrote these in 2025, during a hard season at work. Reading them now, I like most of them and I'd soften "cut without regret." I've learned that some regret is just care that arrived late, and it's worth listening to.
