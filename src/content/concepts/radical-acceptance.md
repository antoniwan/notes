## What it is

Radical acceptance comes from Marsha Linehan's dialectical behavior therapy: fully accepting reality as it is, with your mind, heart and body, especially the parts you can't change. Accepting isn't approving. It's stopping the fight against what already happened, so your energy can go to what's next.

The way I use it, it's also a replacement for the scorekeeper: experiencing what's in front of me without automatically grading it, unless a grade is actually needed.

## How it shows up for me

My mind wants to rate everything: the meal, the day, the person, myself. When I drop the rating and just take it in, I suffer a lot less and see a lot more.
