## What it is

Door-closing is answering a real bid for connection with something technically true and emotionally empty: "no," "it's complicated," "ah, okay." Nothing in it is a lie. The channel stays honest, and it also stays closed.

It's quieter than a fight, which is why it's easy to miss.

## How it shows up for me

I do this when I'm tired or guarded. I tell myself I'm being honest, and I am, but I'm also making sure nobody gets in. Opening the door usually just takes one more sentence.
