## What it is

Doctrine as costume is when a principle made for one purpose gets bent to license a grab that was decided in advance. Free speech, religious freedom, property rights, national security: any of them can be worn this way.

The tell is that the doctrine changes and the outcome doesn't. Greed is the constant. The principle is interchangeable.

## How it shows up for me

I try to apply this to my own side too. If I only care about a principle when it gets me what I want, it's not a principle, it's a costume. That's an uncomfortable test, and I fail it sometimes.
