## What it is

Beautiful chaos is a way of meeting disorder as it is. Babies, kids, a full house, life in general: they bring chaos that can't be eliminated, only met. The frame says you don't have to control it before you're allowed to be present in it.

## How it shows up for me

The first day home with a newborn taught me this one. Everything was loud and nothing was on schedule, and the moment I stopped trying to fix it, I could actually enjoy holding him. I still reach for control, I just reach a little later now.
