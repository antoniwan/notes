## What it is

A few people become the source for everyone else's ideas. A scientist publishes a study; others summarize it; far more people read the summaries than the paper. Most of what you consume comes from **signal repeaters**. They're useful, because they widen what you're aware of. But to get the truth and the core of an idea, go to the **signal source**.

Ask what inspired the people you learn from. Who are your role models' role models?

## How it shows up for me

This Field Guide is mostly a signal repeater, and I try to be honest about that by naming where each idea comes from. If something here helps you, follow the origin line and read the real thing.
