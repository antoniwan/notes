## What it is

In medicine, a hallucination is a sensory experience without the outside stimulus that would normally cause it: hearing a voice when nobody is speaking.

In generative AI, "hallucination" is a loose term for output that's false, made up or unsupported by its source: a confident citation to a paper that doesn't exist. Usage varies, and some researchers prefer "confabulation."

The two have almost nothing in common except the word, and mixing them up makes both conversations worse.

## How it shows up for me

When a tool invents a source for me, I don't think of it as the tool "seeing things." I think of it as a very fluent guess. That keeps me checking, which is the whole point.
