## What it is

Clarence Birdseye noticed that trout caught while ice-fishing tasted remarkably fresh when thawed. That observation became a decade of experiments, and eventually frozen food as we know it. Steven Johnson calls this a "slow hunch": not a flash of insight, but an idea that develops gradually, shaped by many unrelated experiences, and stays alive because someone keeps feeding it.

## How it shows up for me

Some of my best projects started as a sentence in a notes app years earlier. I've stopped deleting old hunches. I just let them sit, and visit them now and then.
