## What it is

The supremacist engine isn't any one bigotry. It's the structural move underneath them: sorting humanity into a worthy in-group and a disposable remainder. Specific bigotries are just instances. The sorting is the engine.

That's why it survives changes of costume. Swap "race" for "intelligence" or "productivity," and the engine keeps running.

## How it shows up for me

I catch it in myself when I'm tired and judgmental: "people like that." It's quieter than hate, and it's the same machine. Dignity from birth is my answer to it. Nobody gets sorted out.
