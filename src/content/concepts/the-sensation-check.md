## What it is

The sensation check is a way of teaching that asks a child to notice a physical difference for themselves instead of accepting a statement from you. Not "breathing slowly calms you down," but "breathe slow three times. Does it feel different?"

It's the only teaching format I know that keeps working when no adult is in the room, because the evidence lives in the kid's own body.

## How it shows up for me

My kids push back on almost everything I say. They don't push back on what they feel. So I've started turning lessons into small experiments, and they remember those.
