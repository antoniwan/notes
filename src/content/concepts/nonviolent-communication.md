## What it is

Marshall Rosenberg's _Nonviolent Communication_ describes a way of speaking and listening with four parts:

1. **Observation:** what happened, without evaluation.
2. **Feeling:** what you feel about it.
3. **Need:** the need behind the feeling.
4. **Request:** a clear, doable action, asked for, not demanded.

"You never help" is an evaluation. "The dishes were in the sink this morning, I felt overwhelmed, I need some help with the house, would you do them tonight?" is all four.

## How it shows up for me

A close friend read the book in two days and said it helped him enormously. My own use is rougher. The trap I've fallen into is using its vocabulary as a weapon, naming the other person's needs for them. The four steps only work when they're about me.
