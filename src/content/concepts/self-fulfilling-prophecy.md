## What it is

Robert K. Merton coined the term in 1948: "in the beginning, a false definition of the situation evoking a new behavior which makes the originally false conception come true." His example was a bank run: a rumor that a healthy bank is failing makes everyone pull their money, and then it fails.

The key is that it starts false. The belief is what makes it true.

## How it shows up for me

I see it most in how I talk about my kids. If I decide one of them is "the difficult one," I start treating them that way, and they start living up to it. So I try to be careful with the labels I say out loud.
