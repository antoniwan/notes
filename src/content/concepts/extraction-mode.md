## What it is

Extraction mode is a way of being in a conversation: listening closely, pulling out the signal, testing your own reactions quietly, and then returning a clean, filtered answer. It's efficient and often impressive.

The risk is a kind of strategic loneliness. If nobody ever sees the raw process, nobody ever really meets you.

## How it shows up for me

This is my default, especially at work. People get my conclusions and never my doubts. I'm learning to show a bit more of the messy middle, even though it feels less competent.
