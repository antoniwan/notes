## What it is

Adverse childhood experiences, or ACEs, are what the CDC calls "preventable, potentially traumatic events" before age eighteen. The standard survey counts eight kinds, one point each: physical abuse, emotional abuse, sexual abuse, witnessing violence between partners, substance abuse at home, mental illness at home, parents separating or divorcing, and a household member in prison.

In survey data from 2011 to 2020, 63.9% of U.S. adults reported at least one, and 17.3% reported four or more.

The limit matters: a point means something happened, not that it left a wound of a certain size. People with the same score can carry very different things.

## How it shows up for me

It helped me to see how common this is. Most people around me are carrying at least one point, and that makes me a little gentler with them, and with myself. It also makes me pay attention to the ones my own kids could be collecting right now.
