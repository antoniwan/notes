## What it is

Charlie Munger told a story, probably apocryphal, about Max Planck touring Germany giving the same lecture. His chauffeur memorized it and offered to give it once. He did fine, until someone asked a hard question. "I'm surprised you'd ask something so simple," the chauffeur said. "My chauffeur will answer it."

**Planck knowledge** belongs to people who paid their dues and really understand. **Chauffeur knowledge** is the ability to repeat information without that understanding. It can sound exactly the same, until the follow-up question.

## How it shows up for me

With AI, chauffeur knowledge is cheaper than ever, for me too. My test is whether I can answer the second question without looking anything up. If I can't, I don't really know it yet.
