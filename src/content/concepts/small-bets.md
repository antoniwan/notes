## What it is

After Enron collapsed and 9/11 nearly sank his airline, Richard Branson built Virgin differently: hundreds of separate companies, each standing on its own legs, linked only by the brand. A series of small, independent bets, insulated from the shocks nobody controls.

Small bets let you learn cheaply, survive the ones that fail, and double down on the ones that work.

## How it shows up for me

This site is a small bet. So are most of my side projects. None of them can sink me, and every one of them teaches me something.
