## What it is

Co-regulation is how two people adjust each other's emotional and physical state through back and forth: voice, breath, touch, pace. It's most visible between a caregiver and a child. A calm adult lends a child their nervous system until the child can do it alone.

Self-regulation grows out of co-regulation. Nobody learns to calm down by being told to.

## How it shows up for me

When one of my kids is melting down, my words matter less than my breathing. If I get loud, they get louder. If I get slow and low, they usually come down with me. It works on adults too, which is humbling.
