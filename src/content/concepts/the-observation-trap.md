## What it is

The observation trap is the belief that seeing a system clearly puts you outside it. You can describe consumer culture perfectly while standing in a mall with shopping bags. You're still participating, whatever you're aware of or intend.

A related trick: feeling you've transcended something, when really you've just already had enough of it.

## How it shows up for me

I caught myself people-watching at a mall at Christmas, thinking "an entire society based on consumption," while buying presents. Then I started defending myself in my own head. That defense was the tell.
