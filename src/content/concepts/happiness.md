## What it is

Happiness gets confused with two things it isn't. It isn't pleasure, and it isn't a baseline you reach and keep. Three ideas help me hold it.

**It comes in moments.** You're a happy person because you have moments of happiness, and as you mature, you catch more of them. Every moment offers a small glimmer, and the glimmer is given, not produced. Your effort goes into noticing it, not earning it.

**It grows on peace.** Peace and happiness get used as synonyms, and I think they're different. Peace is a ground state: steady, quiet, not needing anything to change. Happiness comes in spikes, and when you chase it, it can turn into a performance. Focus on peace, and happiness tends to show up on its own, without the grasping.

**It's coherence.** I wrote this down in November 2025: "Happiness, universally, isn't pleasure. It is alignment." When what you believe, what you say and what you do line up, there's a kind of peace that survives even hard days. It's why discipline, love and meaning can make people feel deeply at peace even while they're suffering, and why pleasure without alignment can feel so empty.

## How it shows up for me

I worked out the peace part on a January 1st, which feels right. I'd spent years chasing happy moments and feeling empty between them. Aiming for peace made the space between the spikes livable.

The trap for me is approving the glimmer later, at my nightly gratitude practice, instead of receiving it in the moment. I'm trying to catch it while it's happening. And when I'm unhappy, the first thing I check now is where I'm out of line with myself. I think every human being can live this one. It doesn't need money or luck, just honesty about what you value and the nerve to act like it.
