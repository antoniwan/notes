## What it is

The usual meaning of perfection, no flaws at all, is impossible, and chasing it mostly produces anxiety. A more useful meaning is completion: doing a thing fully, with attention and integrity, the way a river flows without fighting itself.

The question worth asking is whether you're practicing toward mastery or punishing yourself for not being enough. Those can look the same from the outside.

## How it shows up for me

I've used "perfect" as a whip on myself for years. When I swap it for "complete," I finish more things and enjoy them more. The work gets better too, which surprised me.
