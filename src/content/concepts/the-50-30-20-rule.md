## What it is

The 50/30/20 rule splits take-home pay, what's left after taxes, into three parts:

- **50% for needs:** housing, utilities, groceries, transportation, insurance, minimum debt payments, child care.
- **30% for wants:** eating out, travel, subscriptions, hobbies.
- **20% for savings,** and for paying down debt beyond the minimums.

Elizabeth Warren and her daughter Amelia Warren Tyagi made it popular in _All Your Worth_ (2005). Whether they invented it is debated; they certainly popularized it.

## How it shows up for me

I like it as a first map, not a law. In a high-cost year, needs eat more than half, and that's information too: it tells me where the pressure is before I start blaming myself.
