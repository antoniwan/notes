## What it is

PayPal first tried money transfers between PalmPilots, a big idea for a scattered audience. It took off when it focused on eBay's PowerSellers, a small group of heavy users all in one place. Within three months it served a quarter of them. Peter Thiel's lesson: the perfect first market is a small group of particular people, concentrated together, served by few or no competitors.

## How it shows up for me

This site isn't for everyone, and that's fine. I'd rather write something really useful for a few people than something forgettable for many.
