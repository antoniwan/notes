## What it is

A **soft limit** is a constraint you can push through with more time, better strategy, new skills or resources. Most "limits" in life turn out to be soft.

A **hard limit** can't be overcome by any amount of effort, skill or will. It's structurally or biologically impossible, not a gap you haven't closed yet.

**Finding a hard limit** has its own feeling. Not jealousy of someone else, but the physical recognition of meeting an edge to your own agency, often for the first time. Low anger, warmth, sadness, vulnerability, and a strange weight.

## How it shows up for me

For most of my life, every wall was soft. Work harder, learn more, and it moved. The first hard one I hit felt like grief. It also made me kinder to people who've been hitting hard walls their whole lives.
