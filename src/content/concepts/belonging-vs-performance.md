## What it is

**Performance** is showing competence without being present: care used as a technique, an identity built around looking like the right person in the room. **Belonging** is being recognized as you are, without paying that performance tax.

When love was conditional growing up, the brain can learn an equation early on: performance equals love. It works for a while. It's also exhausting, and lonely, because nobody ever meets the person behind the work.

## How it shows up for me

I learned that equation young, and I still catch myself running it. A hug that's really about looking like a good dad is performance pointed at a child.

The correction I'm practicing is truth over image. Less looking right, more being here.
