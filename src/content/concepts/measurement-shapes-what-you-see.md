## What it is

Arthur Eddington told a story about fishermen whose net had holes of a certain size. After studying their catch, they concluded there was a minimum size of fish in the sea. The smaller fish had simply slipped through. Our instruments don't just record reality; they filter it.

The other side is hopeful. In 1887, Charles Vernon Boys wanted very fine glass threads for an experiment, so he fired a crossbow bolt attached to molten glass. The threads were surprisingly strong, and that discovery led toward fiberglass. New ways of measuring often unlock new ways of making.

## How it shows up for me

At work and with money, I try to ask what my metric can't see. A budget that tracks spending but not stress is a net with big holes.
