## What it is

A simple technique for managing anger, often taught to kids:

- **Red:** stop. Recognize what you're feeling.
- **Yellow:** think. Analyze, reason, consider the best response.
- **Green:** respond.

The colors make it easy to remember in the moment, when remembering anything is hardest.

## How it shows up for me

My mom taught it to me in 2013, in a message: _"Se llama técnica de manejo del coraje, se usa el semáforo."_ A few days later she wrote again: _"ante las dificultades usa el semáforo. Realmente funciona."_ She was right. I mostly fail at the red light, but when I hit it, the rest goes fine.
