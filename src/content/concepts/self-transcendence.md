## What it is

Late in his life, Abraham Maslow added a level above self-actualization in his hierarchy of needs: self-transcendence, finding fulfillment in serving something beyond yourself, like other people, a cause or meaning itself. Mark Koltko-Rivera's 2006 paper brought that late addition back into view.

It changes the top of the pyramid from "become your best self" to "give your best self away."

## How it shows up for me

I use it as a lens on my impulses: does this make me more than myself, or does it only protect me? When I'm angry at a tech company, I can just feel contempt, or I can turn it into an essay or a lesson for my kids. The second one is transcendence. The first one is just a mood.
