## What it is

There's a difference between "AI-generated" and "AI-assisted." The first produces something generic. The second is a person using tools to build something specific. A woodworker who uses a CNC router still designed the piece and chose the wood. The machine cuts, but the human decides.

## How it shows up for me

This site is hundreds of commits of specific choices, and a lot of it involved AI. But the choices, what to build, what to skip, what principles to hold, came from me sitting with the project for months, not from a prompt.

The honest test is whether the decisions are mine. When I can't tell you why something is the way it is, it's drifting toward the generic side.
