## What it is

A toy model is a small, invented, complete example that you solve all the way through before you touch the real, messy case. You say the simplification out loud: "this ignores X and Y." Then you check whether the answer survives when you add them back.

Physicists, economists and good teachers all use them. They make it possible to understand something before you're drowning in it.

## How it shows up for me

When a problem at work or at home feels too big, I shrink it: two people instead of seven, one week instead of a year. Solving the tiny version usually shows me what matters in the big one.
