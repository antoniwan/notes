## What it is

There are three things you can do with an instinct or a strong emotion:

- **Suppress it:** force it out of awareness without resolving it. It usually comes back louder.
- **Indulge it:** act on it without reflection or responsibility.
- **Integrate it:** acknowledge it, let it be real, and choose what to do.

Integration is the middle path. You can feel attraction, anger or envy without shame and without compulsion.

## How it shows up for me

I'm learning to name the instinct out loud, even just to myself: "I feel this, and I'm not going to act on it." Naming it takes away most of its power, and none of its honesty.
