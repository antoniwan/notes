## What it is

Being fully where you are, with the person or the task in front of you, instead of halfway somewhere else.

## How it shows up for me

The clearest example I have is brushing my daughter's hair. For a long time it was a chore I tried to finish fast. When I started giving it my full attention, that hour became some of our best time together. Nothing about the task changed. I did.

I'm not present all the time. My phone, my head, and my to-do list all pull at me. Presence is how I practice [Intimidad](/field-guide/values/intimidad), one ordinary moment at a time.
