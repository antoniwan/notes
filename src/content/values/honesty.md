## What it is

Telling the truth. That part is simple to say. The harder part is that honesty starts inside: you can't be honest with other people about things you won't admit to yourself.

## How it shows up for me

For me, the hard part is turning it inward. Catching my own rationalizations. Noticing when I'm keeping score. Choosing honesty as a value is a commitment to practice it, not a claim that I always notice when I'm lying to myself. I don't.

What helps is making it something I can check. When I say something, can I show it? That turned out to matter even in my work with AI agents: when honesty is the foundation, the agent tests and verifies its own work with results, to prove it. I think people work the same way.
