## What it is

Providing what the people around you need to be safe and well: food, a roof, health care, time.

## How it shows up for me

It's one of the oldest instincts I have as a father. When something threatens my family's footing, my first move is to figure out how to cover it, even if it costs me more than my share. I don't think provision is only money, though. Cooking dinner is provision. Showing up is provision.
