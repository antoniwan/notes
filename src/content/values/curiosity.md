## What it is

Wanting to know how something works, why someone did what they did, or what happens if you try it another way. It's not the same as collecting information. Curiosity asks the next question and then goes and looks.

## How it shows up for me

I call it the engine of most of what works for me. Cooking, code, parenting, this site: all of it started with a question I couldn't leave alone. I believe questions are the root of all human knowledge, so I try to keep asking them, even when it's uncomfortable, and especially when I think I already know the answer.
