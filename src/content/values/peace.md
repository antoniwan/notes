## What it is

A settled state, inside and around you, where nothing needs fixing right now.

## How it shows up for me

For me, peace is less a value I pursue and more a state I'm learning to tolerate without filling it. That sounds strange, but it's honest. When things finally get quiet, my first instinct is to start a project, open a laptop, or find a problem. Learning to sit in the quiet, and let it be enough, is the practice.
