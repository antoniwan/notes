## What it is

When pride and practicality disagree, picking the practical thing. Pride asks "how does this make me look?" Pragmatism asks "what actually helps?"

## How it shows up for me

The clearest moments are with family, where the right answer sometimes means giving up something my ego wanted, like being right, or being seen a certain way. It's one of the values I find hardest to live when I'm tired. At 2 a.m., the choice is between helping and lashing out, and pride always votes for lashing out.
