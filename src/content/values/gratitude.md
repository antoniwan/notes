## What it is

Noticing what other people do for you, and telling them.

## How it shows up for me

The easy version is feeling grateful. The harder version is saying "thank you for everything you do" to someone I'm in a difficult season with. That's the one I try to practice. When I'm exhausted, gratitude is one of the first things I drop, and it's one of the things that most changes the room when I don't.
