## What it is

Owning what's yours to answer for: your choices, your messes, and the people who depend on you. It also points forward. Responsibility isn't only cleaning up after the past, it's preparing for what comes next.

## How it shows up for me

As a father, a lot of it is money and time: making sure the people I love are covered, even when it costs me. At 40 it also means things like writing a will, which I joke about but take seriously. Where I have to watch myself is the line between responsibility and control. Taking on everything isn't responsible. It's just heavy.
