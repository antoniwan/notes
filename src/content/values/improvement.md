## What it is

Getting a little better at something, on purpose, over time. The key words for me are continuous, directional, and not perfectionist. It's about the direction you're facing, not about being done.

## How it shows up for me

I apply it to three things: my craft, my health, and my character. The trap is turning it into a scoreboard where nothing is ever good enough. That's perfectionism wearing improvement's clothes. So I try to measure against who I was last month, not against an ideal I'll never reach.
