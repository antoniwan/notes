## What it is

Moving from thinking to doing. Most of life changes because someone acted, not because someone understood.

## How it shows up for me

I'm a doer, and I trust action more than talk. But I added a limit to this value: action without presence or integrity is drag. Doing a lot fast, in the wrong spirit, creates more work for everyone, including me. So the question isn't only "did I act?" It's also "how did I act?"
