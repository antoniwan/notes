Banana bread of mashed bananas, oat milk, honey, oats, and flour. Mix all the ingredients until combined. Bake at 350°F for 20 to 30 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 3 bananas, mashed
- 1 cup oat milk
- ¾ cup honey
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1½ cups flour
- 1 cup oats
- 2 tablespoons oil, preferably

## Method

1. Mix all the ingredients until combined.
2. Bake at 350°F for approximately 20 to 30 minutes, checking as it cooks.

## Notes

- Watch the center.
