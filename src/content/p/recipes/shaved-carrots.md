Carrot shavings tossed with flour, cinnamon, and confectioner's sugar. Air fryer at 375°F for 5 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 2 carrots
- 2 teaspoons flour
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 2 teaspoons confectioner's sugar

## Equipment

- Air fryer

## Method

1. Shave the carrots and dry them with a towel.
2. Mix the flour, cinnamon, and confectioner's sugar in a large bowl.
3. Add the dry carrot shavings and mix.
4. Put the carrots in an air-fryer basket and cook 5 minutes at 375°F.
