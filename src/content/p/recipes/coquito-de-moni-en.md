Coquito of condensed milk, evaporated milk, cream of coconut, yolks, vanilla, cinnamon, rum, and brandy. Mix and bottle. Yields 4 to 5 large bottles.

## Ingredients

- 4 cans condensed milk
- 4 cans evaporated milk
- 2 cans Goya cream of coconut
- 12 egg yolks, beaten with a little evaporated milk and strained
- 4 tablespoons vanilla
- 4 tablespoons cinnamon
- 1 full bottle of rum
- 1/2 bottle Felipe II brandy, or 3/4

## Equipment

- Funnel
- 4 to 5 large bottles

## Method

1. Beat the 12 yolks with a little of the evaporated milk and strain them.
2. Mix everything.
3. Pour into bottles with a funnel.

## Notes

- Cuca's recipe, Mia's maternal grandmother.
