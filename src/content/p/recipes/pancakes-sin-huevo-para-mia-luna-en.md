Egg-free pancakes of flour, baking powder, cinnamon, oil, and water or oat milk. Cook in a pan over medium heat.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon sugar; can be substituted
- 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon oil
- 1 cup water or oat milk

## Equipment

- Bowl
- Frying pan

## Method

1. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl.
2. Heat a pan over medium heat with a teaspoon of oil.
3. Add the oil and the water or oat milk to the dry ingredients.
4. Mix until even. Do not overmix.
5. Cook the pancakes as usual.
